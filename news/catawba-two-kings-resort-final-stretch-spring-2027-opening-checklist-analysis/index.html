Two Kings Resort's Final Stretch: What Must Happen Before Spring 2027

With the 24-story tower framed and interiors underway, the Catawba Nation's Kings Mountain build moves into the phase most likely to set the real opening date.

By Sarah BegayPublished November 20, 20265 min read

Six months after the Catawba Nation opened the introductory casino at its Kings Mountain site, the permanent Catawba Two Kings Casino Resort has entered the final stretch of construction. A November 19 construction update from 500 Nations describes active work on the upper casino levels, the parking structures and the 24-story hotel tower, with interior and finishing work increasing. No significant delays have been publicly reported. Public updates still point to a spring 2027 opening for the full $1.25 billion resort, though no specific date has been announced.

That last point matters. On large resort projects the final six months, when a structure becomes a working building, are often when opening dates are actually decided. This analysis looks at what the Catawba Two Kings resort must still complete, and why the order of those steps matters for one of the largest tribal builds on the East Coast.

Where the Catawba Two Kings Casino Resort stands

The scale is already established. Structural steel contractor SteelFab has reported supplying more than 27,000 tons of steel for a project listed at about 2.1 million square feet, and a topping-out milestone on the hotel. Ragona Architecture & Design has reported building information modeling work and about 180,000 square feet of raised-access flooring in the casino areas. That type of floor carries power and data cabling beneath slot banks, which makes it easier to rearrange the gaming floor later.

When complete, the resort is planned to hold roughly 4,300 slot machines, about 100 table games, a 385-room hotel and 11 dining outlets, according to the Catawba Nation and its partners. The introductory casino that opened in May, which we covered in our report on the phase one opening, offers a fraction of that: 1,350 slots and 22 live tables. Our September report on the hotel tower milestone covers the earlier structural work.

The opening checklist for a tribal resort

Finishing construction is only the first of several requirements. A tribal resort of this size typically has to clear four overlapping sets of steps, and each can affect the schedule.

Building completion and life-safety sign-off. Fire suppression, alarm systems, elevators and emergency egress all have to be tested and certified before occupancy. On trust land, the tribe's own codes and inspection process usually apply. Most tribal-state compacts also require facilities to meet recognized building and safety standards. In a 24-story tower with hundreds of rooms, elevator commissioning and fire-system testing take a long time and are hard to compress.

Gaming licensing and regulatory review. IGRA requires every place where gaming takes place to be licensed by the tribe. National Indian Gaming Commission rules also require notice to the commission before a tribe issues a new facility license. Our explainer on facility licensing under 25 CFR Part 559 covers the mechanics, including the 120-day notice that remains in force after the NIGC withdrew a rule that would have repealed it. Whether a large expansion at an existing site needs a new facility license depends on how the tribe's ordinance defines the facility. Either way, the tribal gaming regulators will need to review surveillance coverage, cage and count rooms, and internal controls for a much larger floor.

Device delivery and certification. Moving from 1,350 slots to about 4,300 means buying, shipping, installing and testing roughly 3,000 more machines. Each game must meet the technical standards required by tribal regulators and the compact. That usually involves independent lab certification and on-site verification, as described in our explainer on how tribal casino games are certified. Manufacturer production schedules and lab capacity can become bottlenecks when several large properties open in the same season.

Workforce. Opening a casino with 100 tables, 11 restaurants and a 385-room hotel takes a large workforce, with dealers, cooks, housekeepers, security and surveillance staff among the hardest positions to fill. Every key employee and primary management official also needs a background investigation and a gaming license. Hiring and licensing usually begin months before opening, and in a tight regional labor market they can take longer than construction.

Steel sets the outline of the schedule. Licensing, devices and hiring set the opening day.

Why the order matters for the Charlotte market

The Catawba Nation has some flexibility that a first-time operator would not. The introductory casino is open and earning revenue, so the tribe is not under pressure to open the permanent resort before it is ready. Tribal resorts are often opened in stages, with the casino expansion first and hotel floors or restaurants following, and the Catawba Nation has already used a phased approach on this site.

The commercial reasons to open the full property are still strong. Kings Mountain sits on Interstate 85 about 35 miles west of Charlotte, in a part of the Carolinas where the closest large tribal competitor, the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians, operates in the mountains several hours west. Each month the full resort is open gives the tribe more hotel room nights, convention business and table-game revenue that the smaller casino cannot capture. Gaming partner Delaware North and the tribe will also want the opening to fall before the warm-weather travel season.

For the Catawba Nation, the opening also completes a long effort. The tribe's path to gaming in North Carolina ran through federal trust-land approval, litigation and a tribal-state compact, background covered in our explainer on land claims settlement acts and tribal gaming. The resort will be the tribe's largest economic asset by a wide margin.

What to watch

Several public signals usually come before a firm opening date: job fairs and large hiring announcements, device delivery and floor-installation milestones, temporary occupancy approvals for sections of the hotel, and soft-opening or preview events. When the Catawba Nation and Delaware North announce those, a specific opening date usually follows soon after. Until then, spring 2027 remains the public target.
